Smarter Audio Player

One audiobook library across your phone, your NAS, and your cloud drive.

Smarter Audio Player is an Android app for people whose audiobooks and long-form audio live in more than one place — on the phone, on a home NAS, on Google Drive, or on an SFTP server. Add each source once, and the app browses, scans and streams the lot as a single library, with bookmarks, per-book playback speed, a sleep timer, and playback resume across every source.

Storage sources

This device

Pick a folder on internal storage, an SD card, or a USB drive.

SMB / CIFS

Windows shares, UGREEN, Synology, TrueNAS, most consumer NAS boxes.

WebDAV

Nextcloud, Synology, UGREEN Cloud, or any PROPFIND-compliant server.

SFTP & FTP

SFTP with trust-on-first-use host key pinning; FTP with random-access reads.

Google Drive

Sign in with your Google account; browse your Drive folders from the app.

Features

  • Bookshelf sorted by New / Started / Finished with per-book progress.
  • Bookmarks with optional notes; jump back to any spot in any book.
  • Per-book playback speed, skip-silence, volume boost, rewind-on-resume.
  • Sleep timer that keeps running when the screen is closed.
  • On-device chunk cache so seeks are instant once a region has been played.
  • Lock-screen and notification playback controls; Android Auto compatible.
  • No accounts, no telemetry, no ads.
